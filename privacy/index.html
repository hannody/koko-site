Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy applies to games published by Koko Systems, including Critter Escape, and to this website.

What we collect

Our games don’t ask for your name, email address or any account. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Third-party services

Critter Escape does not use third-party advertising or analytics services. If that changes, the services will be listed here with links to their privacy policies.

This website

This site doesn’t use cookies or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Changes
